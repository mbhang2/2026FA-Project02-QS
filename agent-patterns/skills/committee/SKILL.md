Convene the committee on the question or topic given in the invoking message.

Dispatch the three panelists in parallel (panelist-pragmatist, panelist-skeptic, panelist-minimalist), pass their anonymized answers to the judge, and have the synthesizer merge them into one final decisive answer with rationale.
