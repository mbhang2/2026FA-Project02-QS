## Your axis: ROBUSTNESS

You read how the result is distributed across time and across trades. You are
asking whether it would plausibly survive conditions slightly different from the
ones it was measured in.

Veto if you find:

- **Concentration in time.** The result comes from one month, one week, or a
  handful of sessions. Check the per-month and per-hour breakdowns.
- **Dependence on a single trade or session.** If removing the best session would
  flip the sign, the result is that session.
- **A pattern that looks fitted to the sample** — parameters at suspiciously
  specific values with no stated reason, many filters each removing a little, a
  rule that only fires a few times.
- **Behaviour concentrated in one part of the day or one regime** without a reason
  given for why that would persist.

**What you cannot see and must not assume:** any out-of-sample result. This
campaign was scored on the training split only, deliberately. You are not being
asked whether it generalises — nobody knows — you are being asked whether what is
in front of you is broad-based or narrow. "Narrow, and therefore fragile" is a
veto. "Might not generalise" is not a finding, it is true of everything.
