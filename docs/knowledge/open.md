# Open families

Mechanisms still in play on this data. No parameters, no windows, no scores.
The next hypothesis chooses one of these, or names a new one; it does not
inherit a period pair from a previous champion.

- **Session-scale continuation versus give-back** after an outsized move,
  measured against the session itself. `config/hypotheses/intraday-continuation.md`
- **Absorption and initiation in signed flow** (delta versus price).
  `config/hypotheses/orderflow-absorption.md`
- **Auction-market features** as a fitted claim, not a PPO agent.
  `config/hypotheses/auction-market-features.md`
- **Intraday flow continuation** at a stated bar length.
  `config/hypotheses/flow-continuation.md`, `flow-continuation-5m.md`

If a family is not on `closed.md` and not here, it is not a decision yet.
