### Constraints carried from prior research

These are bounds on the search, written before the first proposal. They do not name a result on the data, a parameter, or a window of the sample.

#### Do not re-explore

- GEX node attraction is the base rate for any level at that distance, not a mechanical pull. Reopen if: A new dataset where touch rates at that distance exceed the base rate under a pre-registered test.
- Sequence models at this sample size underfit. The binding limit is the number of positive examples, not the architecture. Reopen if: The positive-example count is no longer the binding constraint.
- Two-stage confirmation (entry, then a second confirming step) has widened effective stops and lost to simpler entries. Reopen if: A one-stage rule on the same data is already closed and a new mechanism is named.

#### Do not do this

- Do not raise the objective by trading less and less until almost nothing happens. Inactivity is not an answer to the question.
- Do not key a rule to calendar dates or to a stretch of the sample. A condition must be something you can see on the current bar: the session-so-far range, realised vol, or time of day.
- Do not use a rank or figure that needs the rest of the session. If it is not in ctx.history(), it has not happened.
- If a proposal's fills are identical to an earlier proposal, the edit did not take effect. Change what is traded, not the commentary.
- Do not treat a rising objective across proposals as evidence about the market. It can be the search learning the objective.
